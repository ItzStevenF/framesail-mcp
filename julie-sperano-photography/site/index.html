Canandaigua & the Finger Lakes, New York People · Dogs & animals · The outdoors

Honest pictures of the people, the animals & the wild places you love.

Portrait, pet and outdoor photography for families, couples, dogs and the animals in your life, made slowly and in good light.
See the work

I photograph what is real: a child leaning into a parent, a dog mid-shake at the water’s edge, the light going gold across the lake.

Sessions are relaxed and unhurried, outdoors wherever we can, in places that mean something to you. You don’t need to know how to pose, and your dog doesn’t need to sit still.

The aim is simple: photographs you’ll still want on the wall in twenty years.

How a session unfolds

Four simple steps
  1. 01

    Say hello

    Send a note through the inquiry form with who (or what) you’d like photographed. I reply to every message personally.

  2. 02

    Plan it together

    We talk through the people and animals involved, a place you love, and the time of day when the light is kindest.

  3. 03

    The session

    Unposed and unhurried. We walk, play, wait for the light. Patience (and treats, for the four-legged) are part of the plan.

  4. 04

    Your photographs
